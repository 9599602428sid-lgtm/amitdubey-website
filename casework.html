Public study & panel brief

Hardening India’s population-scale contact-tracing platform

In 2020, Amit Dubey publicly shared findings from his own study of Aarogya Setu—India’s national COVID-19 contact-tracing application. He flagged identity-access management gaps, manipulable client-side verification, location spoofing risks and SSL-pinning concerns, and noted those issues were subsequently fixed as the platform scaled under live pressure.
